## Stack

## Users

- Men 40+ looking for a urologist (prostate, sexual health), often postponing the first consult.
- Women looking for gynecology and preventive care.
- Patients from Vitória da Conquista and the surrounding region, who travel to the clinic.

They arrive by Google search or social links, usually on a phone, and the job is to confirm the clinic is trustworthy and book a consult by WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

Marketing and booking site for Urolaser Especialidades Médicas (URO LASER LTDA, CNPJ 27.839.278/0001-03), a clinic in Vitória da Conquista - BA. Success is a patient messaging the clinic on WhatsApp to schedule, and the clinic being found and cited in local search and AI answers.

Confirmed differentiators: multidisciplinary team in the same place, the director's training, welcoming care, laser surgery. ## Product Principles

1. Trust before conversion: credentials and registration are visible wherever a doctor is named.
2. One clear action: booking by WhatsApp is always within reach on mobile.
3. Plain, respectful language about sensitive topics; no fear-based or promise-based claims.
4. Every factual claim on the page is real and verifiable; absent proof is left out.
5. Answers first: content is structured so search engines and AI assistants can quote it correctly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Patient base includes older men, so legible type sizes and strong contrast matter; mobile-first use.
